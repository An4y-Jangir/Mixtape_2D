# Brag Plan: Mixtape 2D

## What is this app?
Mixtape 2D is an authentic, skeuomorphic 90s Cassette Deck & J-Card Studio built for the browser, featuring ad-free YouTube Music background streaming, tactile hardware controls, real-time VU needle ballistics, spinning spool ribbon physics, and handwritten 3-panel J-Card customization.

## The angle
In an era of disposable algorithmic playlists and automated shuffle queues, Mixtape 2D brings back the deliberate, tactile joy of handcrafting a real cassette tape for someone you care about.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, punchy declaration: "Streaming algorithms stole the soul of music." Cut to: "Remember when making a mixtape took real effort?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Skeuomorphic Deck & Ribbon Physics**: The onyx tape deck clicks into play; magnetic ribbon spins between spools as dual VU needles swing with real-time audio ballistics.
- **Unfolded 3-Panel J-Card Studio**: Real handwriting on vintage paper stock, highlighter track badges, and holographic foil stickers stamped directly onto the fold.
- **Ad-Free YouTube Music Engine**: Full tracks, real tape duration capacity limits (C-30 to C-90), Dolby B-NR filtering, and instant shareable hash URLs.

## Outro / punchline
The finished tape clicks into its jewel case: "MIXTAPE 2D. Build Something Worth Rewinding."

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry / Playback**: Insert a custom cassette into the deck, click `[▶ PLAY]`, see dual spinning spools and needle ballistics bounce.
2. **Creation / Customization**: Switch to J-Card Studio, pick handwriting style, curate tracks from the YouTube catalog, and place holographic foil stickers.
3. **Sharing**: Unfold the 3-panel J-Card, generate a one-click shareable URL, and export an HD printable inlay.
